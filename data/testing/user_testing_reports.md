# User testing reports, anonymised

Five people ran the QA test on the live site. I ran it first, on 18 October 2026, as an owner check that isn't counted in the results. Then on 20 October four independent testers I know personally, from a range of backgrounds including health, science and data, ran it. Each tester sent me their report by email from the test panel's final screen when they finished. The original emails are held privately by me; testers' names and email addresses have been replaced with T1–T4, and one sentence that named a third person has been removed. Everything else is copied word for word. The summary is in `user_testing_results.csv`.

**What the test covered.** The test checked whether the site as a whole was clear and easy to find your way around. Testers started in the Plain view, where the dashboard opens; the six tasks then took them into the **Detailed view** (the full technical dashboard: the Active measure list, the area card with its rank range, the map key and the ethnicity note), the "Question this work" route, and the "In short" summary on *How certain are the rankings?*. Testers used a laptop or desktop and rated each task Green (easy), Amber (some trouble) or Red (hard).

The six questions asked:

1. Find an area you know on the map and click it. Is it clear how many years of good health people there can expect?
2. Is it clear that the rank isn't exact, and what range of places it could fall in?
3. Is it clear what the map colours now show?
4. Is it clear why the site uses six broad ethnicity groups instead of more detailed ones?
5. Is it clear how you could challenge or correct something you think is wrong?
6. From that summary, is it clear that some areas are definitely among the lowest, while others can't be placed for certain?
